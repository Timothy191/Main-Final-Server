## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

Establish the top-level compliance framework for the computerized systems that
support Arch Systems' mining operations. This document maps applicable
regulatory and management-system requirements to the in-scope systems,
classifies system criticality, defines the validation strategy per system, and
sets the governance structure that sustains the validated/compliant state.

It is the single compliance blueprint for the operational software stack and is
the baseline for internal audits, inspection readiness, and change control.

### 1.2 Regulatory Scope

This is a **mining / industrial operations** environment, **not** a GxP-regulated
(pharma/clinical) one. The applicable frameworks are therefore industrial and
management-system standards, not 21 CFR Part 11 / EU Annex 11:

| Framework                                                | Relevance to this environment                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ISO 45001:2018** — OHSMS                               | Mining is high-risk; operational systems record safety-relevant events (machine breakdowns, delays, incidents).                                                                                                                        |
| **IEC 61511:2017 / IEC 61508** — Functional safety (SIS) | Applies to the _actual_ safety-instrumented and equipment-control systems on site. The systems in scope here **record and report** operational data; they are **not** safety-instrumented systems themselves (see §10, assumption A3). |
| **ISA/IEC 62443** — IACS cybersecurity                   | The operational stack is industrial automation-adjacent; OT/IACS cybersecurity controls apply.                                                                                                                                         |
| **ISO 27001:2022** — ISMS                                | General information security management for the IT estate.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **ISO 9001:2015** — QMS                                  | Production process control and records (tonnage, shift logs, batch/lot-equivalent production records).                                                                                                                                 |
| **ISO 14001:2015** — EMS                                 | Environmental records (overburden, topsoil, parting movement) where captured by the portal.                                                                                                                                            |
| **ALCOA+ data integrity**                                | Production tonnage / BCM data feeds royalty, tax, and regulatory production reports — data integrity is a regulatory and financial-reporting control.                                                                                  |
| **Site-specific mining regulations**                     | Jurisdiction-dependent: e.g. MSHA (US), WHS (Mines) Regulation (AU), MHSA (ZA), Quarries Regulations (UK). **To be confirmed — see §10, A1.**                                                                                          |
| **SOX / financial reporting controls**                   | Applicable **only if** the operating entity is publicly listed and production data feeds external financial statements. **Assumed not applicable — see §10, A2.**                                                                      |

### 1.3 Organisational Scope

- **Organisation:** Arch Systems (mining operations).
- **Site:** Single mine site with a control room (open-cut; commodities include
  coal, ore, overburden, topsoil, parting — per the portal's material taxonomy).
- **Departments in scope:** Control Room, Production, Drilling, Safety,
  Engineering, IT.
- **Systems in scope:** The custom software stack supporting control-room
  operations and production reporting (§2). Physical equipment, PLCs, SCADA, and
  site safety-instrumented systems are **out of scope** for this document except
  where the in-scope software interfaces with them.

### 1.4 Out of Scope

- Physical mining equipment and its embedded control firmware.
- Safety-instrumented systems (SIS) and their independent verification (governed
  by IEC 61511 process-hazard-analysis, separate from this document).
- HR, finance, and corporate IT systems not handling operational/production data.

| Tier                             | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                 | Validation Required                                          | Examples                          |

GAMP 5 (second edition) categories are pharma-origin but the software-classification
lens generalises to industrial CSV and is retained here. The category describes

| SYS-002   | NestJS Backend   | 5 — Custom Application         | Internally developed service                                    | High — full lifecycle                          |
| SYS-003   | Supabase         | 4 — Configured Product         | COTS Postgres + Auth with RLS policies and schema configuration | Medium-High                                    |
| SYS-004   | Redis            | 3 — Non-configured COTS        | Used as-is with operational config                              | Low-Medium — verify configuration              |
| SYS-005   | Ollama           | 3 — Non-configured COTS        | Off-the-shelf model runtime                                     | Low — verify installation & model              |
| SYS-006   | Rust Telemetry   | 5 — Custom Application         | Internally developed plugin                                     | Medium-High — full lifecycle (smaller surface) |

**Mixed-category note:** Where a Category 4 product has custom add-ons (e.g. a
Supabase edge function or a custom Postgres extension), classify the custom
portion as Category 5 and the base as Category 4.

**Risk acceptance:** If stakeholders push for reduced validation of a
Safety/Production-Critical system, document the risk acceptance with QA
sign-off — do not silently reduce scope.

## 10. Assumptions to Confirm Before Approval

These assumptions were made to produce the draft and **must be validated** by the
System Owners / Quality before this document is approved. Each maps to a
remediation item in §11.

- **A1 — Jurisdiction / site mining regulations.** The specific mining safety &
  operational regulations (MSHA / WHS-Mines / MHSA / Quarries Regs / other) were
  not provided. The matrix uses a placeholder. _Action: confirm jurisdiction and
  populate §5 site-reg rows._
- **A2 — SOX applicability.** Assumed the operating entity is **not** publicly
  listed, so SOX/IPE controls over production-volume data are out of scope.
  _Action: confirm; if listed, add SOX IPE controls to SYS-002/SYS-003._
- **A3 — The in-scope stack is not a Safety-Instrumented System.** The portal,
  backend, and telemetry record and report operational data; they do not perform
  safety interlocks. IEC 61511/61508 therefore applies to the site SIS, not this
  stack, and is held in §5 as an _interface agreement_ only. _Action: confirm no
  in-scope system performs a safety function; if any does, it must enter the IEC
  61511 SIL lifecycle and be reclassified._
- **A4 — Inventory completeness.** The inventory is derived from this repository
  only. Adjacent operational systems (SCADA historians, weighbridge, fleet GPS,
  drone/survey tools) that exchange data with the portal are not listed.
  _Action: System Owners to surface integration partners; add to §2 and classify._
- **A5 — Ollama is assistive only.** Classified Non-regulated on the assumption
  its output does not inform safety or production/royalty decisions.
  _Action: confirm; if output informs decisions, reclassify to Supporting and
  add ALCOA+/change controls._
- **A6 — Rust telemetry is not the sole monitor of safety-critical equipment.**
  Classified Supporting. _Action: confirm; if it is the sole source of
  safety-critical equipment state, reclassify to Safety/Production-Critical and
  apply IEC 61508._
- **A7 — Environmental data capture.** §5 maps ISO 14001 only to the extent the
  portal captures environmental records (overburden/topsoil/parting movement).
  _Action: confirm whether environmental reporting is a regulated output; if so,
  expand ISO 14001 controls._
- **A8 — User counts.** §2 user figures are estimates. _Action: System Owners to
  confirm for access-control provisioning (ISO 27001 A.5/A.8)._
